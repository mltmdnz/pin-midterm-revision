Reading Text

First Day Nerves: Three People Share Their Stories

Leo, 24 – Starting a First Office Job

"I remember my first day at a marketing agency in London last year. I was incredibly nervous because I didn't know anyone, and I was worried about making a good impression. To be safe, I decided to wear a smart suit and arrive twenty minutes early. However, when I walked into the office, everyone was wearing jeans and T-shirts! My manager laughed kindly and told me that the dress code was completely casual. At first, I felt a bit embarrassed, but after having coffee with the team, I started to relax. By the end of the week, I realized that everyone was friendly and always ready to help."

Hannah, 27 – Moving to a New Country to Teach

"Two years ago, I moved to Tokyo to teach English, even though I couldn't speak Japanese at all. Before my first class, my hands were shaking! I had to teach a group of twenty adult students, and I was afraid of forgetting my lesson plan. Instead of starting with grammar rules, I decided to play a simple speaking game so we could learn each other's names. The students loved it, and the atmosphere became much warmer immediately. Looking back, moving abroad was the best decision I've ever made, although living in such a crowded city took some time to get used to."

Sam, 22 – Joining a University Orchestra

"I've played the violin since I was eight, so I thought joining the university orchestra would be easy. I was wrong! When I went to my first rehearsal in October, the other musicians were playing much faster and more confidently than me. During the break, I nearly decided to leave because I didn't feel good enough. Fortunately, the girl sitting next to me noticed I was stressed and offered to practice the difficult parts together after class. Thanks to her help, I didn't give up, and now playing in the orchestra is the happiest part of my week."
